How it works

  1. 1
    Run a prompt in an open ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Grok tab.
    CitoSkeleton reads the responses your browser has already received — it never sends a request of its own.
  2. 2
    Open Analyze for the full breakdown of that one answer.
    Fan-out queries, cited vs. merely fetched sources, products, places and brand mentions.
  3. 3
    Set up a Campaign to track a brand across a fixed prompt set.
    Share of Voice, visibility, average rank and per-prompt results, measured the same way every run.
  4. 4
    Use Compare to watch one prompt move over time.
    Which brands and domains gained or lost ground between runs of the same question.

Brand detection is fully automatic for every industry — there is nothing to configure. Your brand, competitors, prompts and tracked engines live per campaign, in the Campaigns tab.
