How it works

Open ChatGPT or Gemini in a browser tab and run a prompt. This extension passively inspects network responses already received by your browser, extracts search fan-out queries, citations, and product entities, and stores them on your local device only.

Raw captures are kept locally so analytics can be re-derived without re-running requests.
